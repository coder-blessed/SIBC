Welcome to SIBA

Students in Blockchain Africa (SIBA) is a pan-African community dedicated to fostering blockchain education, innovation and adoption among students at every academic level. We close the gap between Africa's rapidly growing Web3 ecosystem and students seeking structured learning, mentorship and real opportunities.

Vision

A future where every African student, regardless of academic background or location, has access to blockchain education and opportunities to build within Web3—driving innovation, inclusion and economic growth across the continent.

Mission

Create a supportive pan-African student community that provides structured blockchain education, hands-on project experience, mentorship and pathways into the Web3 industry.

Community

Whether you're a complete beginner or already building on-chain, there's a place for you in SIBA through workshops, hackathons, networking events and campus chapters across Africa.
