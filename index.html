Welcome to SIBA

Students in Blockchain Africa (SIBA) is a pan-African community dedicated to fostering blockchain education, innovation, and adoption among students from secondary school to postgraduate level. We close the gap between Africa’s growing Web3 ecosystem and students seeking structured learning, mentorship, and opportunities.

Vision

Access to blockchain education for every African student.

Driving innovation, inclusion & economic growth.

Mission

Build a supportive student community with mentorship and hands-on learning.

Pathways into the Web3 industry.

Community

Workshops, hackathons, networking, and campus chapters.

A place for beginners and builders alike.
